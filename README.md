Necesse mod: Expand your settlement to pre-1.0 size before any bosses and at negligible cost.

Settlements now start at 80x80 tiles (5x5 regions), down from 300x300 tiles (the size of a map cell pre-1.0). This mod adds seven expansions which bring the total size to 304x304 tiles (19x19 regions) at the cost of 1 coin per expansion. Six additional expansions can then be purchased at the vanilla prices, bringing the max size to 496x496 tiles (31x31 regions).
